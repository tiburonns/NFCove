# NFCove Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

NFCove is designed to process NFC content locally on your iPhone.

## Data collection

NFCove does not require an NFCove account and does not operate an NFCove server that receives your NFC tag contents. The current release does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

## NFC data

When you choose to scan a compatible NFC tag, NFCove uses Apple's Core NFC framework to read the tag. If you save a scan, NFCove stores the displayed tag information and, when available, the original NDEF record fields locally on your device.

When you choose to write a tag, NFCove sends the NDEF content you selected to the compatible tag and may immediately read it back to verify the result.

## Local storage

NFCove stores app preferences, created cards, and saved scans locally in the app's container on your device. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information unless iOS preserves it through a device backup.

## Opening content in other apps

When you explicitly choose to use saved content, NFCove may ask iOS to open a URL, email address, phone number, SMS destination, or map location in another app. The receiving app and service are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Tracking and advertising

NFCove does not use cross-app tracking and does not show third-party advertising in the current release.

## Changes

If a future NFCove release adds online accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Support link on NFCove's App Store product page.
